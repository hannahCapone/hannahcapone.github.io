A little bit about me:

I grew up in Winthrop, Massachusetts, a small seaside town just outside Boston, and now live in New York City.

I studied Computer Science at Fordham University, graduating summa cum laude in May 2024 with a 3.9 GPA, a minor in Visual Arts, and a concentration in Graphic Design. I received Fordham's Jogues Scholarship, was a member of the Alpha Sigma Nu Jesuit Honor Society, and spent a semester studying at the University of Westminster in London.

On campus I tutored Computer Science through Fordham's Higher Education Opportunity Program and was involved in Girls Who Code, the Computer Science Society, Women in STEM, and SPIRE, a women's empowerment club.

I've always lived somewhere between engineering and art, which is probably why forward deployed work suits me: it's half building, half people, and every customer is a new problem to design around.

Outside of work, I'm usually taking film photos, scrapbooking, reading, or traveling.
