About Me

I'm a Forward Deployed AI Engineer at PolyAI in New York, where I build and deploy agentic voice assistants for enterprise clients across healthcare, financial services, and transportation.

I joined PolyAI in September 2024 and was promoted to Forward Deployed AI Engineer IV in March 2026. I'm the Healthcare Pod's primary engineer for proof-of-concepts and fast-paced deployments, and the first point of contact for telephony integrations. Day to day, that means leading SIP integrations with contact center platforms, building integrations between client systems and PolyAI's Agent Studio in Python, and owning delivery for large enterprise deployments handling over a million calls a month. I also build internal tooling with PolyAI's agent orchestration team, including Wren for Slack, which lets people edit voice agents straight from Slack threads.

Skills: Python, C++, Java, Go, SQL, REST APIs, AWS, Datadog · SIP, VoIP, and CCaaS platforms like Twilio, Genesys, and AWS Connect · Conversational AI, MCP, RAG, prompt engineering, and ASR/TTS.

I graduated from Fordham University in May 2024 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a 3.9 GPA, including a semester studying abroad at the University of Westminster in London. Before PolyAI, I was a Software Engineer Intern at toTree and tutored Computer Science I & II in C++ through Fordham's Higher Education Opportunity Program (HEOP).

I'm from Winthrop, Massachusetts, a small suburb near Boston. Outside of work, I'm a proud Celtics fan and love expressing my creativity through film photography, scrapbooking, painting, and drawing.

Go Rams!
